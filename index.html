Human-smoke candidate

Hybrid inference, deliberately staged.

Standalone tooling for pinned llama.cpp and KTransformers/SGLang-KT profiles on AVX-512/VNNI CPUs with optional PMem-aware and NVIDIA paths.

Current state

0.0.1 established the repository. The 0.0.2 candidate publishes exact-commit target runners for human smoke; the full dual-runtime/MoE milestone remains 0.1.0.

Model boundary

This project does not distribute or download models. Operators provision models outside the checkout; future commands may inspect an existing local path read-only.

Compute contract

AVX-512 and AVX-512 VNNI are the only universal compute gates. PMem, reserves, and NVIDIA capabilities are discovered or operator-selected.
